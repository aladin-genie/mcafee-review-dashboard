## 🎓 SUPPORT TEAM TRAINING PRIORITIES

### Response Quality Issues (Teachable Moments)

#### ❌ DON'T: HIGH RATING + APOLOGY
**Example:** 5★ review saying "problems solved" → "We're concerned about your experience..."

**✅ DO:** Thank them and ask for referral
> "Hi [Name], we're thrilled your issues are resolved! Reviews like yours inspire our team. Would you recommend McAfee to friends? 🛡️"

---

#### ❌ DON'T: LOW RATING + NO EMPATHY
**Example:** 1★ VPN crash → "Thanks for letting us know..."

**✅ DO:** Acknowledge frustration + specific steps
> "Hi [Name], we sincerely apologize for the VPN crashes. This isn't the experience we want. Please try: 1) Restart VPN 2) Check for app update 3) Contact our tech team at 1-866-622-3911. We're here to help! 🔧"

---

#### ❌ DON'T: GENERIC "CONTACT SUPPORT"
**Example:** Any issue → "Please contact our support team..."

**✅ DO:** Provide 2-3 troubleshooting steps first
> "Hi [Name], let's try these quick fixes: 1) [Specific step] 2) [Specific step] 3) If still issues, contact us at [link]. Most problems resolve with step 1! 💡"
